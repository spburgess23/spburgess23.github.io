<h1>About</h1>

I'm SP Burgess, and this site collects my projects. The big one at the
moment is **Bole Hill Quarry** — capturing its buildings with
photogrammetry and publishing them here as walkable 3D meshes. A pipeline
on the server does the heavy lifting: meshing, clean-up, web optimisation
— the website just consumes its output, and a new building goes live with
a single command.

Everything else I make along the way lands in the **Projects** section.
The whole site is static — no backend, no database, no accounts — built
to be fast and to stay standing.
